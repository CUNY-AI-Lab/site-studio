About

I research climate adaptation strategies in urban coastal communities. My work combines field ecology, GIS mapping, and community-based participatory research to understand how cities can build resilience to sea-level rise and extreme weather events.

Prior to joining the faculty in 2020, I completed postdoctoral research at the Urban Climate Lab and earned my PhD from Columbia University. I teach courses in environmental science, urban ecology, and climate change adaptation.
