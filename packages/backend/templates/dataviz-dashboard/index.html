Methodology & Notes

Rent burden is defined as households paying more than 30% of income on rent. Severe rent burden is defined as paying more than 50% of income on rent.

Data is drawn from the NYC Housing & Vacancy Survey, conducted every three years by the U.S. Census Bureau in partnership with NYC Department of Housing Preservation and Development. Sample size: approximately 18,000 housing units.

All dollar amounts are adjusted for inflation to 2023 dollars using the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U).
