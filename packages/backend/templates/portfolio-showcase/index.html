About

I'm a UX researcher and designer focused on creating inclusive digital experiences through participatory research methods. My work centers on understanding how technology can better serve diverse communities, particularly those traditionally underserved by mainstream design practices.

With a background in Human-Computer Interaction and Community Engagement, I've spent the past 8 years working with public sector organizations, nonprofits, and civic tech initiatives. My research combines qualitative methods with collaborative design to ensure that the voices of end users shape the tools they use.
