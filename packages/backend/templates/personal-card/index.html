About

I am a cognitive psychologist studying how people learn, remember, and make decisions. My research combines behavioral experiments with computational modeling to understand the mechanisms underlying human memory and learning.

Before joining Columbia, I completed my PhD at Stanford University and was a postdoctoral fellow at MIT's Department of Brain and Cognitive Sciences.

Research Interests

  • Memory consolidation and retrieval
  • Computational models of learning
  • Decision-making under uncertainty
  • Educational applications of cognitive science
