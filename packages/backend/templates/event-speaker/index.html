Digital Humanities Colloquium

Exploring the intersection of technology, culture, and scholarship

April 5-6, 2024 Queens College, CUNY
Register Now

About the Colloquium

Join leading scholars, librarians, and practitioners for two days of presentations and discussions on digital methods in humanities research. Topics include text mining, digital archives, data visualization, and computational analysis of cultural materials.

Featured Speakers

Keynote Speaker

Dr. Amanda Chen

Director, Digital Scholarship Lab | Stanford University

"Digital Archives and Public Memory: Challenges and Opportunities"

Dr. Chen is a leading figure in digital humanities, with expertise in digital archives, cultural analytics, and public history. Her recent book, Digitizing the Past, explores how digital technologies are transforming historical research and public engagement with history. She has led major digitization projects including the Asian American Digital Archive and the Oral History Commons.

Friday, April 5 | 9:00 AM - 10:30 AM

Dr. James Rodriguez

Associate Professor of English
University of Michigan

"Text Mining Victorian Literature: Methods and Findings"

Specializes in computational literary analysis and 19th-century British literature. Creator of the Victorian Networks Project.

Leads digital collection development and provides support for DH research projects. Expertise in metadata, digital preservation, and open access.
