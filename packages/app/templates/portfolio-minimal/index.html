About

Alex Kim is a visual artist and photographer based in Brooklyn, New York. Their work explores the intersection of urban environments, memory, and technological change.

Kim holds an MFA in Photography and Related Media from Parsons School of Design and a BA in Art History from Yale University. Their work has been exhibited at MoMA PS1, the Brooklyn Museum, and the San Francisco Museum of Modern Art.

Recent projects have been supported by the Guggenheim Fellowship, the MacArthur Foundation, and the National Endowment for the Arts.
